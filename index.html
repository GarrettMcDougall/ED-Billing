Ultrasound
J149US-guided aspiration / I&D (professional component)$56.45
Covers real-time ultrasound guidance for a bedside aspiration, drainage, or biopsy you perform yourself — e.g. an abscess I&D or joint aspiration under direct US visualization. Two components exist: Technical ($47.30, for owning/operating the equipment) and Professional ($56.45, for performing/interpreting). As a hospital-based ED physician using hospital equipment, bill the Professional component only. Don't bill this separately for routine diagnostic or procedural POCUS (see H100).
H100POCUS (emergency department investigative ultrasound)$19.65
Only eligible when: (1) rendered personally by an ED physician meeting training/experience standards; (2) a radiologist is not available for urgent interpretation; and (3) clinically suspecting one of five life-threatening conditions — pericardial tamponade, cardiac standstill, intraperitoneal haemorrhage (trauma), ruptured AAA, or ruptured ectopic pregnancy. Requires a permanent image record AND an interpretive report. Maximum 2 services per patient per day (2nd only as a follow-up for the same condition). Not for general/undifferentiated POCUS use.
